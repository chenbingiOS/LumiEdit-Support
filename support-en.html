Help & support

1. Choose a photo from the home screen, or start with the Crop or Adjust card. Only the selected image is opened. Live Photos and animated images are edited as a still frame. 2. In the editor, open Adjust and choose exposure, brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature, tint or sharpness. Use the slider to change its value. Hold Compare to view the original; Undo and Redo restore complete edit steps. 3. Open Crop to choose a centered ratio, move the frame or drag its corners freely. Rotate turns the photo counterclockwise by 90 degrees. Free and square crops preserve the selected area where possible; other fixed ratios recenter the crop. Apply commits one step; Cancel discards the crop changes. 4. Tap the top save button to open the save page. Choose JPEG, HEIC or PNG and Standard or Full size. JPEG and HEIC offer three quality levels. Standard output has a long edge of at most 2048 pixels. Full size keeps the cropped working image size. Input images larger than 4096 pixels on the long edge are resized during import. 5. Save to Photos requests permission only to add a new photo. If denied, use Share or allow access in system Settings. Your original photo is never overwritten. Sharing passes a temporary copy to the destination you choose.

Troubleshooting If a photo cannot open, confirm it is available from iCloud and select it again. If an adjustment preview is unavailable, retry the adjustment or use Undo. Failed export or sharing preparation keeps your edits so you can retry. Keep the app open until output completes. Sessions are not automatically saved as projects; leaving the editor with unsaved edits asks whether to discard them.

When contacting support, include the app version and device model and describe the steps that led to the issue. You do not need to send private photos.

Photo access: When opening the photo picker, allow selected photos or your full library. The album menu switches albums; when editing, tap a photo to open it directly in the editor. Cancel returns home. If access was denied, enable it in Settings.

Home screen and camera Tap Edit to choose a photo, or Camera to take one. Camera access is requested only after you tap Camera; if access is denied, you can change it in system Settings or continue editing photos. Captures are not automatically saved. Quick tools provides Crop and Adjust.

Home cover Adding and changing home covers is no longer available. Covers saved in an earlier version can still be displayed and remain separate from photo editing sessions.

Editing photos: tap a photo to open it directly in the editor, without a preview or second confirmation.

Settings provides app language, photo and camera permissions, feedback, terms, privacy and app information. Cover management is no longer available. Deleting the app removes any old local cover copy without changing the original photo.
