Lumi Edit Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Lumi Edit is an independent photo editor provided by the developer listed on its App Store product page.

Photos and editing You browse and choose an image in the app’s photo picker, using Apple Photos APIs. Editing takes place on your device. The app does not send your photos, edit parameters, or photo metadata to a developer-operated server. This release does not analyze faces or create face templates. Images are processed as still photos, with a working long-edge limit of 4096 pixels.

Permissions Opening the photo picker requests access to Photos. You can allow selected photos or your full library; the picker only displays photos you allow. You can change this access in Settings. When you choose Save to Photos, the app requests permission to add a new photo if needed. It does not overwrite or delete the original. If you refuse, you may keep editing or use system sharing.

Storage and deletion Your editing session is kept in memory and is not an automatically saved project. Closing the editor discards the session. Sharing creates a protected temporary file; the app removes it when sharing completes or is cancelled. A file left after an abnormal exit may remain until the next sharing operation, when files older than one day are removed, or until iOS clears temporary storage or you delete the app. Saved copies in Photos remain until you delete them in Photos. Exported files do not copy the source location or camera metadata.

Sharing and Apple services System sharing sends a photo only to destinations you choose. Those destinations have their own policies. Apple Photos may retrieve an image from iCloud according to your system settings. These system services are separate from developer-operated image processing.

Other data This release has no account, advertising, tracking, analytics, subscriptions or developer-operated cloud service. The app does not automatically send personal data to the developer. System diagnostics may be managed by Apple under your device settings. Editing and layout libraries run locally. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Support and this website If you choose to email support, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send, solely to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Private photos are not required. Correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to handle the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligation. You can use the contact below to request access, correction or deletion of information you sent to support. We do not have a server-side copy of your locally edited photos to retrieve or delete.

The public policy and support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them sends ordinary web request information, such as your IP address and browser information, to the hosting provider. We add no analytics, tracking scripts or advertising cookies. GitHub and the email provider process their service information under their own policies, potentially outside your country. Reading the policy bundled inside the app does not load the website.

Questions and changes See Help & support in Settings and the App Support link on the App Store product page for support. Material policy changes will be reflected in the app's privacy policy.

Camera and home cover The camera is requested only when you tap Camera on the home screen. Captured photos enter the editor and are not automatically saved to Photos. A home cover is optional: the app cannot read your system wallpaper. Tap a photo in the picker to set the cover directly; it is centered to fill the screen. You can remove it in Settings by choosing Remove cover and confirming. The selected cover is kept as a protected local JPEG copy, with a long edge up to 2048 pixels and without source location or camera metadata. This copy is excluded from device backups. Removing the cover deletes the local copy and restores the compact home screen; the original photo is unchanged.
